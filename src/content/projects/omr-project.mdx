## What it does

Reads music notation through a camera and converts it into structured output,
using an SSD detector to locate notation elements and an LSTM to interpret them
in sequence. Camera feedback is integrated into the loop rather than applied as
a post-processing pass, and the system ships with its own GUI and control
interface.
