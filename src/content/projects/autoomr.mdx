## What it does

The optomotor reflex (OMR) is a subconscious head rotation present in the
majority of complex sight-seeing animals, and the frequency of OMR stimulation
can be used to quantify one's vision. For my Hillman Academy summer project, I
developed AutoOMR, a neural-network driven prototype to autonomously stimulate
and detect OMR, and sold this prototype to a Stony Brook University lab for
$12,000.
